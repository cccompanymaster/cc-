FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Questions you might have before reaching out.

What is the minimum contract length?

Standard contracts start from 3 months. One-off campaigns (launches, events, review responses) can run on 1-month terms, and long-term partnerships are offered in 6 or 12-month options.

What is the minimum budget needed?

It varies by industry and goals, but a light-weight package starts around KRW 1.5M / month. Regulated industries (medical, legal) may incur additional review fees. We will transparently outline achievable goals by budget tier in the first consultation.

How does medical ad review work?

Noah Marketing Lab handles review processes at each association (Korean Medical Association, Korean Dental Association, Korean Oriental Medicine Association, etc.). We consider passing likelihood from the creative planning stage and target approval within 7–14 days on average.

What are the early termination terms?

Termination is allowed after the notice period specified in the contract (typically 30 days written notice). Media spend and production costs already incurred will be settled, but no penalty is charged. For new clients, we can discuss a first-month performance-based early exit option.

Can you serve clients outside Seoul / Gyeonggi?

Yes — anywhere in Korea. Our HQ is in Songdo, Incheon (301 Incheon Tower-daero), but we default to online meetings and video reporting, so there is no regional restriction. On-site visits can be scheduled as needed.

How will I receive performance reports?

Dedicated managers send weekly and monthly reports, and you also receive access to the real-time dashboard (run by Noah Database). We interpret results using business KPIs like revenue conversion, CPL, and repeat-purchase rate.

Can we run agencies in parallel?

Yes. However, duplicate bidding or keyword conflicts within the same channel can reduce ROI, so we will structure a channel split in the first meeting. Under NDA, sharing your existing agency data enables a more precise proposal.

What does a consultation include?

A diagnostic meeting of up to 60 minutes covering industry, goals, and budget, followed by a draft tailored proposal. Provided regardless of whether you contract with us, and all content is kept confidential.
